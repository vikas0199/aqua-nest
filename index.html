About AQUA NEST

Refreshing India Since 2009

AQUA NEST has been a trusted name in India's beverage industry for over 15 years, driven by a clear mission to deliver pure, refreshing, and high-quality drinks that enhance everyday life. Through continuous innovation and an unwavering commitment to excellence, the brand has grown from a small-scale operation into a pan-India presence, serving millions of satisfied customers across both urban and rural markets.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified
FSSAI Approved
10M+ Happy Customers

State-of-the-Art Production Facilities

Our world-class manufacturing facilities use cutting-edge technology and automated production lines to ensure hygiene, consistency, and superior quality in every bottle. Supported by rigorous multi-stage quality control and over 50 checks conducted throughout the production cycle, every product meets international standards and consistently exceeds customer expectations.

Advanced Laboratory Testing

Comprehensive quality analysis at every production stage

100% Automated Quality Control

Zero compromise on hygiene and safety standards

Pan-India Presence & Reach

AQUA NEST's extensive distribution network spans major cities and rural areas, supported by modern logistics, temperature-controlled warehouses, and regional hubs. Partnering with over 1,000 distributors and retailers, we ensure freshness, timely delivery, and wide availability for millions of households every day.

We are committed to environmental sustainability through eco-friendly initiatives such as water conservation, renewable energy use, and advanced recycling systems that reduce our carbon footprint. Driven by innovation, our dedicated R&D team continuously develops improved formulations and sustainable packaging solutions, ensuring product excellence while minimizing environmental impact.
